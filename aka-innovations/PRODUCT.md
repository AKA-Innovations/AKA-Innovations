# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary Users:
- School Directors & Principals (decision makers needing high-level oversight, instant reports, and compliance control).
- Teachers & Administrative Staff (executing daily attendance, gradebook entries, timetable changes, and parent communication).
- Parents & Students (receiving real-time updates, fee receipts, homework alerts via WhatsApp/App).

## Product Purpose

AKA Innovations Agentic School ERP is a category-defining education operating system that unifies traditional dashboard management (forms, tables, reports) with natural-language Agentic AI execution. It eliminates manual administrative bloat, reducing 14-step workflows into 1 conversational command.

## Positioning

"The School ERP That Works Both Ways": Traditional administrative dashboards when you need structured tables; autonomous AI agents when you want tasks executed instantly in plain English.

## Operating Context

Busy K-12 schools and educational institutions. Administrators using web desktops, teachers managing classrooms on mobile devices, parents receiving mobile push/WhatsApp notifications.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Dual Operating Engine**: Seamless toggle between classic ERP dashboards and conversational AI agent prompt bar.
- **Autonomous Agent Workforce**: Dedicated agents for Teacher, Principal, Accounts, Exam Controller, and Parent Liaison roles.
- **Role-Based Security & Governance**: Strict audit logs, human-in-the-loop approvals for sensitive tasks (e.g. fee waivers, grade modifications).
- **High-Performance UI**: Sub-200ms snappy spring interactions, hardware-accelerated transforms, zero layout shifts.

## Brand Commitments

- Product-first, zero-fluff engineering identity by AKA Innovations.
- High visual craft: Dark obsidian command-center aesthetic, crisp typography (Plus Jakarta Sans + JetBrains Mono), tactile feedback.

## Product Principles

1. **Demonstrate, Don't Claim**: Show live interactive AI executions rather than static bullet lists.
2. **Snappy & Tactile**: Every interaction responds under 180ms with explicit active feedback (`scale(0.97)`).
3. **Dual-Mode Freedom**: Never force AI where structured tables work better; provide instant toggling.
